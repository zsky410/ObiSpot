# ObiSpot MVP Scope Freeze (Day 1)

## Muc tieu MVP
Chot mot luong demo end-to-end on dinh cho he sinh thai dat san:
- Customer dang nhap -> chon slot -> tao booking.
- Admin xac nhan/huy booking va bat/tat slot.
- Customer thay trang thai booking cap nhat.
- Chatbot tra loi FAQ va goi y slot theo du lieu that.

## Must-have (MVP 2 tuan)
- Auth co ban: dang ky/dang nhap/dang xuat (customer), dang nhap (admin).
- Danh sach venue/field va slot trong theo ngay.
- Tao booking va xem booking history.
- Admin booking queue: list/filter co ban, confirm/cancel.
- Admin slot control: available/blocked.
- Dashboard toi thieu: booking hom nay + ti le slot da dat.
- Chatbot: FAQ + goi y slot retrieval-first, co fallback khi AI loi.

## Out of scope (Post-MVP)
- Thanh toan online that.
- Push notification, email that.
- Voucher/loyalty, danh gia san, ghep doi.
- Dashboard nang cao nhieu bieu do.
- Chatbot tu dong tao booking.

## Quy tac freeze pham vi
- Khong them feature moi trong 2 tuan neu khong thuoc luong demo chinh.
- Moi request ngoai scope dua vao backlog `post-mvp`.
- Uu tien fix blocker cua demo path truoc moi polish UI.
